process-group cleanup and CPU/address-space/file limits for trusted installed code.

atomic registration of related objects; frozen evaluation sets; and retrievable,
content-addressed validation records bound to run/result/dataset digests. API and
CLI expose the new workflows. No biological engine or clinical claim is added.

License revision 1.1.0 explicitly permits genuine free humanitarian work and
preserves the owner's exercise/licensing of rights he actually holds, including
commercially. Existing grants, third-party and contributor rights remain intact.
The custom license remains legally unreviewed and source-available. Reference
descriptors advance to 0.3.0 for metadata; engine algorithms remain at 0.1.0.
