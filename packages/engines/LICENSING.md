# Licensing and commercial-use policy

Current distribution: **Peykar 0.3.0**.

The project owner requests public source access and noncommercial collaboration,
while reserving commercial exploitation. Peykar therefore uses a **project-specific
source-available license**, not an OSI-approved open-source license.

## Operative documents

- `LICENSE`: Peykar Noncommercial Source License 1.1.0, the proposed terms offered
  for original material in this distribution. **This is a custom, legally unreviewed
  draft**, not an official PolyForm license or a claim of court-tested enforceability.
- `NOTICE`: project/licensor notices and the historical-license boundary.
- `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt`: the original license of the 0.1.0 baseline.
- `licenses/baseline-0.1.0.json`: baseline commit and original per-file checksums.
- `docs/licensing-rationale.md`: alternatives, policy examples and limits.

The current original project distribution uses the custom SPDX reference:

```text
LicenseRef-Peykar-Noncommercial-1.1.0
```

A license reference identifies this project's text; it does not indicate SPDX/OSI
approval or create copyright ownership. Third-party components remain separately
licensed. The old Apache text is included for historical notice and existing grants,
not as a new alternative permission for the entire current distribution.

## Critical historical limit

The private Peykar 0.1.0 snapshot already supplied in this conversation contained
Apache-2.0 terms. No GitHub publication or third-party distribution is established
here. **Any valid rights actually granted to a recipient under Apache cannot be
withdrawn by this change.** Such a recipient may continue using the material covered
by their grant, including commercially, subject to the original terms.

If no third party obtained an earlier grant and the necessary original rights are
held, a different license can be chosen before public distribution. A private draft
or a file name alone is not evidence that every third party has obtained a grant.
The current snapshot offers its original material under the custom terms, preserving
any valid prior permissions. It cannot guarantee that an earlier recipient will be
barred from using the earlier material. Do not rewrite history to hide that issue.

`licenses/baseline-0.1.0.json` records the earlier exact snapshot. Current reference
model/data packages have new versions for the licensing metadata change; historical
0.1.0 descriptors are not overwritten or silently offered as new restricted versions.
The underlying reference algorithms are scientifically unchanged.

## Public-use policy, subject to that historical limit

| Activity requiring rights under the current public grant | Policy under LICENSE |
|---|---|
| Personal study and noncommercial experiments | Permitted with conditions |
| Genuine nonprofit teaching or public-benefit research | Permitted with conditions |
| Genuine humanitarian relief offered to recipients free of charge | Expressly permitted under section 2, with conditions |
| Unconditional donations/general grants paying ordinary humanitarian staff salaries | Permitted if they do not buy access, priority or a commercial deliverable |
| Free noncommercial forks and source redistribution | Permitted with attribution and source availability |
| Public noncommercial use funded by donations/grants | May be permitted if funding does not buy a commercial deliverable or special access |
| Selling copies, subscriptions or access | No public license grant |
| Paid API, SaaS or hosted simulation service | No public license grant |
| Paid simulation, analysis or consulting deliverables | No public license grant |
| For-profit internal product development/R&D using covered material | No public license grant |
| A nonprofit charging specifically for access/results/services using covered material | No public license grant |
| An open-source project using covered material for a commercial purpose | No automatic exemption |

The operative definitions are in LICENSE. This table is not a separate license and
cannot override statutory rights, existing Apache grants, or third-party licenses.
The public license grants no commercial rights; an additional grant requires a
separate agreement signed by the relevant rights holder. There is no automatic
future conversion to a permissive license and no commercial trial exception.

## Owner use and commercial agreements

Section 7A explicitly preserves **Sina Mirshafiei's use of rights he owns**. He can
run his own material for personal research, HomBon, a commercial product or a paid
service without buying a license from himself. He may separately license those
rights to others. No new payment or self-licensing obligation is created here.

A legally separate business needs an applicable ownership basis or written grant,
even if the owner controls it; that grant can be royalty-free. This does not give
him rights over other contributors' material, external models, datasets or engines.
Audit those rights before promising a commercial license for the complete system.

The archived `licenses/Peykar-Noncommercial-1.0.0.txt` preserves the preceding
noncommercial terms. Valid prior permissions remain effective. Version 1.1.0 makes
humanitarian free services and retained owner rights explicit; it does not relicense
third-party material or retroactively remove any earlier rights.

## Dependencies, outputs and contributions

Third-party packages, engines, datasets, models and their license texts retain their
own terms. This policy does not relicense them. Numerical facts, users' data and
independent findings are not claimed as Peykar-owned merely because the program
was used. Using covered software for a commercial project is nevertheless outside
the current public grant, subject to the historical-license limit above.

Contributors retain ownership of their contributions. Current noncommercial terms
do not automatically give the project owner power to sell contributor-owned code
under another license. A reviewed contributor agreement and rights audit are needed
before offering such commercial licenses. No contributor assignment is presumed.

## Legal readiness

Before public licensing/enforcement, obtain a software/IP lawyer's review of the
custom text, ownership, contribution/employment obligations, AI-assisted authorship,
dependency compatibility and the earlier Apache release. The project owner's
name in a notice is an asserted licensor identity, not proof of exclusive title.
The repository does not establish a guaranteed right to damages or success in court.
