# Central corneal thickness model 1.0.0

Third corneal component, after epithelial wound closure and endothelial cell
density. The model covers how central corneal thickness (CCT) swells after cataract
and other intraocular surgery and returns, and how an edematous cornea thins after
endothelial keratoplasty. Values are fractions of each group's preoperative CCT.
Data: [dataset 2026-10-28](../../data/cornea-thickness-2026-10-28/README.md), with
33 development records (69 series) and 15 sealed records extracted by separate
custodians. Nothing here is a clinical or biological validation claim.

## Model

`peykar.model_learning.thickness_population` (numpy only):

- Kinetics `ln f(t) = ell + e·r(t)`, with `r(t) = (1 + t1/tau)/(1 + t/tau)` and
  `t1` = 1 day. `ell` is the long-term level, `e` the excess over it one day after
  surgery, and `tau` the time scale of a hyperbolic return. On development series
  with at least four follow-ups, the hyperbolic return fitted post-cataract swelling
  better than one exponential phase or power laws with exponent 0.5 or 2. The
  dof-adjusted log-SD for host group means was 0.0094, against 0.0120, 0.0123 and
  0.0105 respectively ([round 0](round0_law_comparison.py)).
- Two families per species:
  - `host` (cataract, glaucoma, phakic-lens and vitreoretinal surgery): the
    patient's own endothelium stays, and levels are relative to the preoperative CCT.
  - `graft` (DSAEK, DMEK, PK, DALK and `other`): the long-term level is pooled as an
    absolute thickness, `ell = L − ln(CCT0/550 µm)`. The preoperative CCT, known at
    surgery, therefore sets the expected long-term fraction: an edematous cornea is
    expected to thin and a thin keratoconic cornea to thicken. This structure comes
    from physiology and was not fitted.
- Given `tau`, the law is linear in `(ell, e)`. Posteriors are exact Gaussian
  mixtures over an 80-point `tau` grid and over noise levels. Quantiles come from
  the exact mixture distribution function, with no sampling.
- Hierarchy per family: family mean, procedure context, study, and series (group
  mean or single eye). Diagonal variances are estimated by penalized REML on
  per-series likelihood summaries; the weak summary prior is subtracted, so
  directions that a series does not inform carry no weight. A procedure without
  training studies falls back to its family.
- Primary forecaster `population_assimilation_robust`: the population prior mixed
  0.8/0.2 with a broad component, then updated exactly by the observed follow-ups.

## Tasks and criteria

The tasks are the same as for endothelium:

- U1 initial state: from context, series kind and preoperative CCT.
- U2 next observation.
- U3 landmark: from the first follow-up only.

Per series, RMSE must be ≤ 5 pp and max error ≤ 10 pp. For U2 and U3, RMSE must
also be at least 0.5 pp below persistence. The calibration windows are 80%
intervals within [0.70, 0.90] and 90% intervals within [0.80, 0.97].

Possibly shared cohorts are handled with
[author-overlap-1.0.0.json](../../data/cornea-thickness-2026-10-28/author-overlap-1.0.0.json),
generated by the generic `scripts/link_study_authors.py`. Development studies that
share authors leave training together. Two sealed studies were trained without
their linked development study.

run. Round 1 used weak-prior moment summaries. Round 2 used likelihood-information
summaries, declared before the run and adopted by the declared rule. The primary
forecaster was chosen by a rule fixed before round 1. Results are in
[`development-loso-2026-10-28/`](development-loso-2026-10-28/tables.md); values of

## One-shot sealed test (2026-10-28)

The test was run once, after freeze commit `deef7664`, with protocol
[`protocol-1.0.0.json`](protocol-1.0.0.json) (SHA `a97db853…`) and every pinned
hash matching. Operator: `scripts/evaluate_thickness_population.py --sealed`, exit
0, 28.2 s, report SHA `62bf7c96…`. Coverage: 15 studies, 29 forecast series (26
host, 3 graft) and 65 next-observation targets. Public artifacts are in
[`sealed-test-2026-10-28/`](sealed-test-2026-10-28/tables.md): figure
`sealed-results.png` and declared analysis `sealed-analysis.json`. Values of six
studies without CC BY/CC0 terms are redacted.

| declared check | result |
|---|---|
| H1 calibration, next observation | 58/65 = 0.892 and 63/65 = 0.969: **both inside**, both near the upper edge (intervals slightly wide) |
| H2 accuracy, next observation | **pass**: 1.60 pp versus persistence 2.06 and last-two trend 22.70 |
| H3 landmark | **pass**: 2.31 versus persistence 4.01 pp; coverage 0.815/0.892 inside both windows |
| H4 initial state, 21 control group means | 18/21 within 5/10 pp; 5.17 pp versus pooled curve 6.82 (host 2.51 versus 1.68: the pooled curve is better there) |
| per series | next observation 28/29 within 5/10 pp, 14/29 also ≥0.5 pp better than persistence; landmark 28/29 and 17/29 |

The following comparisons were reported but not tested:

- Analog kNN (k = 5) did about as well on next observation: 1.60 pp, or 1.60
  against 1.49 for the model on their 59 common targets.
- Analog kNN did better on landmark: 1.47 against 1.66 pp on common targets.
- Assuming return to the preoperative level (f = 1) gave 6.05 pp.

Results by context (model RMSE, then persistence RMSE):

- cataract: 0.96/2.02 pp, 22/22 series within 5/10 pp;
- vitreoretinal: 0.72/4.30 pp (no training study; host-family fallback);
- glaucoma, two single eyes: 1.70/1.47 pp;
- DMEK: 2.24/1.04 pp;
- `other`: 5.40/1.44 pp.

The following failures were seen after unsealing. None was used to change the
frozen model.

- **Laser capsulotomy assigned to the graft family.** Nd:YAG capsulotomy groups
  ([PMC8775002](https://doi.org/10.3390/diagnostics12010150)) were extracted as procedure
  `other`, and the declared rule maps `other` to the graft family. Their CCT stayed
  near 101–103%. The graft-family day-1 excess (+0.33) predicted 124–126% at one week
  before any follow-up, an initial-state error of 17–19 pp, and pulled the
  next-observation forecasts down by 4–6 pp. The `other` category mixes
  endothelial-replacement and minor procedures. A future version should assign the
  family by mechanism, for example by whether the procedure replaces endothelium or
  clears edema, or by a preoperative-CCT rule. It must be tested on newly reserved
  studies.
- **Late thickening after DMEK.** A 10-year DMEK series
  ([PMC9156484](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10792-021-02176-3)) thickened slowly
  from 81% to 88% of its preoperative CCT. The model has no late-thickening
  mechanism, so the forecasts lagged: 2.24 pp against 1.04 pp for persistence.
  The same series showed the late endothelial cell loss that the endothelium model
  tracked slowly, so coupling the two components is the natural next step.
- **Host initial state.** For host initial state the population mean (day-1 excess
  0.127) was above several sealed cataract studies with mild swelling (4–5%). The
  pooled training curve was closer. Part of the curve's advantage at day 1 comes from
  linear interpolation through the reference for studies without day-1 data.
- **Unanswerable contexts.** Graft forecasts (3 series) and single-eye prediction
  are too sparse to support any claim.

Both the development and the sealed sets are now consumed. A further version must
be tested on newly reserved studies.
