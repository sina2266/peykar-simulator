# Additional corneal evidence, 2026-10-27

This is a targeted primary-source/repository search, not an exhaustive survey or
an assertion that more downloaded data validate the simulator. Original bytes,
retrieval receipts, attribution and differing terms remain separate from Peykar's
license. No author was contacted and no remote scientific code was installed.

## Admitted numerical forecasting source

Suárez-Barrio C et al.2019, *Hyaluronic Acid Combined with Serum Rich in Growth
Factors in Corneal Epithelial Defects*,
[DOI10.3390/ijms20071655](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6480555/),
CC BY4.0. Original NCBI-version XML/PDF, metadata, terms and actual Table1 reader
are retained in [the conditioned-forecast experiment](../../vcornea/repair-front/conditioned-forecast/README.md).
Both original files match NCBI MD5. All28 printed mean/SD entries, source cells,
units and times are preserved. Only Control0/24h conditions the new candidate;
the five later Control means are scored after actual child completion.

This is a different assay from untreated repair: PBS plus dexamethasone/
chloramphenicol and diclofenac.33 operated eyes belong to17 rabbits, with delayed
contralateral-eye use; per-arm IDs/n are unknown. Table half-day times conflict
with the methods' daily photograph description. These limits prevent individual
or clinical validation claims. The actual new forecast **failed**, not a data
admission or parser failure. Acquisition receipts describe their acquisition-time
knowledge; later unmasking/results are appended in the experiment report.

## Reserved image evidence, incomplete cohort

Aung TT et al.2025, *Structure–Activity Relationship in ε-Lysine Peptides: The
Length Effects on Antifungal Activity*,
[DOI10.1021/acs.biomac.5c00907](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.biomac.5c00907).
The [original supporting PDF](raw/epsilon-lysine2025-original-bm5c00907_si_001.pdf)
was downloaded from ACS Figshare article30173862/file58121652; its MD5 matches
the primary record. The SI has explicit **CC BY-NC4.0**, retained in its
[primary metadata](raw/epsilon-lysine2025-figshare-metadata.json) and
[acquisition receipt](raw/epsilon-lysine2025-acquisition.json). That statement
is not assigned to the separate main article, whose full bytes were not acquired.

Healthy rabbit PBS wound photographs show four unlabeled rows over eight days,
but the main figure reports six eyes. These32 embedded JPEGs are approximately
242×182pixels each, with no stable animal IDs or independent physical ruler.
They are **not the complete six-eye cohort**. No segmentation, magnification
correction, area extraction, training or accuracy comparison has been performed.
Row identity and image geometry require a separately frozen observation/QC
protocol before use; image visibility is not a numerical ground-truth table.

## Additional aggregate lead, not evaluated

Kibar Kurt and Belge2021,
[DOI10.33988/auvfd.724013](https://doi.org/10.33988/auvfd.724013),
has saline-control aggregates at0/72/96h and reports **SE**, not SD.12 control
eyes belong to six rabbits; injury depth/basal preservation is unresolved.
[Source/context receipt](raw/ankara2021-source-context.json) and primary metadata
are retained. The original PDF remains private with its hash; no current candidate
used these outcomes or claims a passing comparison. Three late timepoints would
not establish early-phase dynamics even with a passing endpoint check.

## Search and reuse limits

[Screening decisions](screening-summary.json) and
[actual requests/responses](raw/repository-search-receipts.json) preserve targeted
HF, Dryad, DataCite/Zenodo and Figshare searches. HF yielded two already-known
corneal candidates and no rabbit-cornea result; neither is this longitudinal
assay. The121 wound-related DataCite dataset records and24 Dryad results are
metadata inspection, not121/24 admitted experimental datasets. Raw metadata
snapshots remain under `raw/`; failed endpoints are not counted as acquisition.

The2019 OCT follow-up DOI10.1111/aos.14053 reuses the earlier Fischak rabbit
experiment; it cannot be counted as another independent experimental cohort.
Printed Dale1994 time-scale/coefficient inconsistencies and its speed-calibration
dependence remain documented in the preceding experiment. Neither an extra
publication nor a more complex ML/RL architecture creates missing observations.

Next useful evidence is stable eye/animal identity, complete longitudinal areas
or calibrated images, acquisition times and injury/care/substrate metadata, with
another study reserved before model selection. The new data remain corneal work;
retinal and other-tissue reuse stays in the platform roadmap.
